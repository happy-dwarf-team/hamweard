**Leave a campaign and you are back in the main menu in seconds, without the game restarting. The dwarf walks home; the forge stays lit.**

Normally **Exit to Menu** in a campaign and **Back** on the country selection screen close EU4 and start it again: the logos, the loading screen, the whole wait. With Hamweard both take you straight to the main menu, usually in about 4 seconds, and **Continue game** points at your latest save.

## What you get
- **No restart** after Exit to Menu or Back: the main menu comes back in the same game.
- **Fewer crashes on the way home:** Hamweard also clears the windows each campaign leaves behind, which in our tests made a later campaign crash.
- **Tested in single player:** twelve campaigns in a row with wars, battles and peace deals, and no crash.
- **Honest limits:** your next campaign starts in a game that already played one. We saw nothing carry over, but Anvil treats Hamweard as a mod that affects gameplay: it is off in multiplayer, off in ironman unless you allow it, and not tested with other mods yet.

## You need
- **[Anvil](https://anvil.happydwarf.dev/) 1.5 or newer** in the game folder. Download it once from its website: the Workshop can't install it for you.
- **EU4 1.37.5** on Steam (Windows). The GOG build is not supported yet.
- Remove the original EU4 Menu Patch first: both change the same part of the game.
- After subscribing, start the game, approve Hamweard in Anvil (Ctrl+Shift+F12, then Mods) and restart. Anvil asks again after every update.
